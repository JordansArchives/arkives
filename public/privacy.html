Arkives

Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Arkives is a workspace for creators to manage brand partnerships: deals, invoices, quotes, scripts, boards, tasks, a calendar, and an inbox for brand emails. This policy explains what data Arkives collects, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have. Arkives does not sell your data and does not show ads.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and password (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us).
  • What you create: profile, rate card, deals, clients, invoices, quotes, timelines, scripts, boards, ideas, tasks and calendar entries you add.
  • Social handles you enter in Settings. If you run a social scan, Arkives fetches the public follower and engagement numbers for those handles.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Calendar in Settings, Arkives asks Google for permission to:

  • See, create, change and delete events on calendars you own, so your Google events appear on the Arkives calendar and you can add Arkives deadlines to Google Calendar.
  • See your free/busy availability, used only for scheduling features you turn on.
  • See your Google account email address, so Settings can show which account is connected.

How Arkives handles this data:

  • Your Google events are fetched when you open the calendar and shown only to you. Arkives does not store copies of your Google events.
  • When you add an Arkives event to Google Calendar, Arkives stores that event's ID so it can remove it if you delete it in Arkives. Arkives only ever deletes events it created.
  • The access Google grants is stored encrypted and is never sent to your browser or shared with anyone.
  • Arkives never reads your Gmail and never requests access to your email messages.

Arkives' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the calendar features you see in Arkives. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never used to train AI models, and never read by people except when you ask us for support, for security reasons, or when required by law.

Brand-deal inbox (optional)

Arkives gives you a private forwarding address. If you set up a filter in your email account to forward brand emails to it, Arkives keeps the sender's name and address, the subject, the date, the message ID, and the first 2,000 characters of the text, so you can sort them in your Inbox. Attachments and the rest of the message are not stored. You can delete any item, or get a new address so the old one stops working.

How we use information

  • To run the features you use and show your own data back to you.
  • To keep Arkives secure and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account when needed.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Where data is stored

Arkives runs on service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, the server code that talks to Google, and receiving forwarded emails.
  • Google: only if you connect Google Calendar.
  • Apify: only if you run a social scan, to fetch public profile numbers.

Each account can only access its own data. Data is encrypted in transit, and Google access is additionally encrypted at rest.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Calendar at any time in Settings › Connections. Arkives revokes its access with Google and deletes the stored access. You can also remove Arkives at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Export your data from Settings › Account.
  • Delete your account and its data by contacting us at the address below.

Retention

Your data is kept while your account is active. Google access is deleted when you disconnect. When you delete your account, its data is deleted from the live database.

Children

Arkives is not intended for anyone under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change, and significant changes will be announced in the app.
